## Description

### Your music. Your vibes. Let's go.

SetCue turns the music already on your computer into a live DJ set. Load two decks, let SetCue match tempos and fade between tracks, and put on a light show while you play — no streaming account, no subscription, no club gear required.

**Try it free first:** every download is the full app with unlimited playback for 14 days. Buy a licence key here when you're ready to keep playing.

👉 **[Download the free demo](https://github.com/Steampunk-Arena/SetCue-releases/releases/latest)**

### Mix like a DJ

- **Two decks** — Now Playing and On Deck, each with its own level and headphone cue.
- **Auto DJ** — tempo-matched auto fades walk the beat from one track into the next. Set the fade length, hit go.
- **Headphone cue on a separate output** — main mix to your speakers, preview the next track in your headphones.
- **Record your mix** to WAV and share it.

### Put on a show

- **20+ visualizer patterns** like Plasma Helix, Car Grid and Mech Grid, with colour palettes and a pop-out window for a second screen. New on Windows: Neon Gateway, Data City, Hyperspace and Holo Terrain scenes, with a speed setting for each pattern.
- **Lyrics and titles** on screen, loaded automatically from LRC files.
- **Music videos** — drop MP4, WebM or MOV files into a set and crossfade between pictures. *(Windows)*
- **DJ lights** — drive DMX-512 lights through a USB-DMX cable; with a music video playing, the lights pick up its colours. *(Windows)*

### Plug in your gear

- **MIDI controllers** — map buttons, knobs, faders and jog wheels with MIDI learn. *(Windows)*
- **Scratching** — vinyl-style scratching on your jog wheels, or drag a spinning record with the mouse. *(Windows)*
- **Keyboard controls** — Space to play/pause, arrow keys for patterns and palettes.

### Build the set

- See every track's BPM, length and the **total set time** at a glance.
- Reorder tracks, save multiple sets, and **import / export sets** to share with friends.
- Plays **MP3, WAV, FLAC, Ogg, Opus, M4A and AAC.**
- No music handy? The free **demo music pack** has 6 original tracks with BPM tags, cover art and lyrics.

### FAQ

**Does it work with Spotify, Apple Music or other streaming apps?**
No — SetCue plays music files you own: your MP3s, Bandcamp purchases, CD rips and downloaded Suno songs. Streaming services don't allow their music in third-party DJ apps.

**What happens when the demo ends?**
Playback stops until you enter a licence key. Your playlists and sets stay saved and editable.

**Windows says "Windows protected your PC."**
SetCue is code-signed, but Windows is cautious with new publishers. Click **More info → Run anyway** after checking the publisher is Stephen Dickey.

**Do I need a DJ controller?**
No. Everything works with a mouse and keyboard; a MIDI controller is optional.
