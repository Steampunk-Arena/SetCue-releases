# SetCue creative brief

**Your music. Your vibes. Let's go.**

## What SetCue is

SetCue is a desktop DJ app for the music already on your computer: your MP3s, Bandcamp purchases, CD rips and downloaded Suno songs. No streaming account, no subscription, no DJ gear needed.

- **Two decks and Auto DJ:** SetCue matches tempos and fades from one song into the next on its own, or you mix by hand with the crossfader.
- **A visualizer for a second screen:** 20+ animated scenes (neon cities, hyperspace, wireframe canyons) in color palettes you choose. Pop it out to a TV or projector.
- **Lyrics on screen:** timed lyrics and song titles over the visuals, karaoke style.
- **Music videos:** mix MP4, WebM and MOV videos; the crossfader blends the pictures too. *(Windows)*
- **DJ lights:** drive real DMX party lights through a USB cable; they change color and move with the beat. *(Windows)*
- **MIDI controllers and scratching:** plug in a DJ controller, or scratch the on-screen record with the mouse. *(Windows)*
- **Record your mix** to a WAV file.

Works on Windows 10/11, macOS 13+ and Linux. Features marked *(Windows)* are in the Windows version now and coming soon to Mac and Linux.

**Price model:** free 14-day demo with every feature, then a one-time licence from getsetcue.com (no subscription).

## Who we're talking to

- People who host: house parties, game nights, birthdays, small bars and events.
- Bedroom DJs and music lovers who want to mix without buying a controller.
- AI-music makers (Suno and similar) who have folders of their own songs and want to play them out.
- Streamers and desk-setup fans who love a glowing second screen.

## The one thing to get across

**Turn the music on your computer into a DJ set with a light show, in minutes.**

## Video ideas

Pick one, or make it your own.

1. **"POV: you're the DJ at your own party."** Drop songs in, hit play, Auto DJ fades the next track in, and the TV lights up with visuals. Cut to friends reacting.
2. **Desk-setup reveal.** Lights off, hit play, and the second screen, DMX lights and RGB gear come alive on the beat.
3. **"I made this song with AI, now watch it play."** Show your Suno song with its lyrics scrolling over the visualizer.
4. **Before / after.** A plain music player and a silent room → SetCue with visuals, lyrics and lights.
5. **60-second tutorial.** Make a set, pick a theme and pattern, press play. Show how fast it is.
6. **Scratch or controller moment.** Scratch the record with the mouse or a DJ controller, then let a big transition land.
7. **Visualizer tour.** Flip through patterns and palettes to one song: "which one is your vibe?"

## Must show

- The SetCue app on screen, clearly readable, for at least a few seconds.
- Music playing **and** the visuals reacting to it.
- At least one transition: Auto DJ fading from one song into the next.
- Say or caption: **"free 14-day demo at getsetcue.com"**, or your Trybe link if you have one.

## Music: please read

**Only use music you have the rights to.** Platforms mute, block or demonetise videos with other people's songs, and we can't use them in ads.

Safe choices:

- **The SetCue demo music pack** (6 original tracks with cover art and lyrics, free on the download page). You're cleared to use these in your SetCue videos.
- Your own songs, including AI songs from a paid plan that gives you commercial rights.
- Royalty-free music you've licensed.

## Style and specs

- **Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080×1920. 15–45 seconds works best.
- **Hook in the first 2 seconds:** start on the visuals or the drop, not on a menu.
- **Show the real screen:** record it with a screen recorder (OBS or the Windows Game Bar, Win + G) and cut that in with phone footage of the room or setup. Bright visuals filmed in a dim room look great.
- **Captions on:** many people watch muted.
- **Be yourself:** honest reactions beat a script.

## Do

- Mention the free 14-day demo, so it's an easy try.
- Point out that it plays music you already own; no streaming or subscription.
- Keep the app version current: download the latest from getsetcue.com.

## Don't

- Don't use copyrighted songs you don't have rights to (see Music).
- Don't say SetCue works with Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube; it plays local files.
- Don't promise features the app doesn't have, or show Windows-only features in a Mac or Linux video.
- Don't show other brands' logos prominently, or lyrics that name other brands.
- Don't use DMX lights or lasers in a way that could hurt anyone. Never aim lasers at people.
