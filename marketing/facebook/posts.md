### 1. Save a playlist (`out/post-1-save-a-playlist.png`)

Build your set once, play it all night. 🎧

1. Click **+ New set** and give it a name.
2. Drag in your songs (or click **+ Add**), then drag rows to put them in order.
3. Done. SetCue saves every set automatically, so it's ready next time you open it.

Want to share a set with a friend? **Export set** saves it to a file, and **Import Set** loads it on their computer (they'll need the same music files).

### 3. Set up DMX lights (`out/post-3-set-up-dmx-lights.png`)

Your lights, on the beat. 💡

SetCue can drive real DJ lights through a USB-DMX cable.

1. Plug the USB-DMX cable into your PC and daisy-chain your lights from it.
2. Open Settings → **Set up DJ lights**, choose your cable, and click **+ Add light** for each light (SetCue suggests the next free address).
3. Switch on **Lights follow the music**: dimmers follow the energy, colors follow the visualizer, and moving heads jump on the beat.

Use **Test channels** to check each light before a show. Works with Open DMX (FTDI) cables like the DSD TECH SH-RS09B and Enttec Open DMX, and with Enttec DMX USB Pro-style adapters.

DJ lights are in the Windows version now; Mac and Linux are coming soon.

### 4. Play music videos (`out/post-4-play-music-videos.png`)

Mix the music. Mix the picture. 🎬

1. Click **+ Add** and pick MP4, WebM or MOV videos, just like songs.
2. When a video plays, its picture takes the visualizer's place, and the crossfader blends one video into the next (or into the visualizer).
3. Pop the picture out to a second screen or TV for the room.

Prefer your visuals on top? Visualizer settings → Music videos → **Mix with the visualizer**.

Music videos are in the Windows version now; Mac and Linux are coming soon.

### 5. Album covers and lyrics with AI (`out/post-5-covers-and-lyrics-with-ai.png`)

No cover? No lyrics? Make them with AI. 🤖🎤

**Album covers**
1. Make a square image with an AI image tool (1000×1000 or bigger works well).
2. Embed it in the song as its front cover with a free tag editor like Mp3tag.
3. In SetCue: Settings → **Refresh album covers**.

**Lyrics**
1. Use an AI transcription tool to time your lyrics, and save them as an `.lrc` file.
2. Name it exactly like the song (e.g. `My Song.lrc` next to `My Song.mp3`).
3. In SetCue: Settings → **Scan lyrics**. If the lines run early or late, nudge them with Lyric sync.

Then switch on **LYRICS** on the visualizer bar and sing along. 🎶
